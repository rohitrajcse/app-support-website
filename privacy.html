Privacy Policy

Metronote Music

Last Updated: 4 June 2026

Metronote Music ("we," "our," or "the app") is a music recording and editing app for iOS.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication manages your session.

  • Email address
  • Display name
  • Authentication identifiers

We do not store passwords on our servers.

Content You Create

  • Music projects
  • Imported audio files
  • Recorded audio
  • Effect presets
  • Exported song metadata

Most creative content stays locally on your device unless exported.

Device Permissions

Permission Why We Ask
Microphone To record audio
Files/Documents Import audio files
Media Library Import audio from device

2. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users
  • Save app sessions
  • Enable recording and editing features
  • Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Data Type Storage
Account Data Firebase / Google
Projects & Audio Local Device

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase / Google Authentication
  • Apple iOS services

5. File Sharing & Export

Exported files may be accessible through Files app or apps selected by users.

6. Data Retention

  • On-device data remains until deleted
  • Firebase account data remains until deletion request

7. Your Rights

  • Sign out anytime
  • Revoke permissions in iOS Settings
  • Delete projects
  • Request account deletion

8. Children's Privacy

Metronote Music is not intended for children under 13.

9. Security

We use trusted services such as Firebase and Google for authentication.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally.
